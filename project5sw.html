Type Posters

Poster I

Featuring the iconic Stormtrooper helmet from the original saga, this poster symbolizes the foundation of Star Wars’ visual identity. Just as the trooper design became timeless, the franchise’s early typefaces—like ITC Serif Gothic and News Gothic—set the stage for a bold and enduring brand universe.

Poster II

Highlighting the Clone Trooper helmet of the prequel era, this poster represents a pivotal chapter in Star Wars history. Typography during this period, including Albertus and other cinematic typefaces, enhanced the epic scale of the storytelling while reinforcing the saga’s distinctive identity.

Poster III

This poster showcases the sleeker, modernized Stormtrooper helmet from the sequel trilogy. Reflecting how typography evolved alongside the saga, fonts such as Trajan and Trade Gothic brought fresh sophistication to Star Wars’ promotional materials, bridging legacy with innovation.
