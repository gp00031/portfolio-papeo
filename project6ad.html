Poster I

Featuring Edvard Munch’s The Scream twisted into an AI generated imitation, this poster reflects the loss of emotional depth in machine made art. The raw human anxiety of the original is replaced with synthetic emptiness, underscoring the campaign’s message: AI does not create it destroys.

Poster II

This poster reimagines Van Gogh’s Starry Night consumed by AI interference, illustrating how technology strips away the painter’s expressive brushwork and emotional resonance. What was once a vivid reflection of the artist’s soul becomes a lifeless pattern of pixels.

Poster III

This poster depicts the Mona Lisa corrupted by AI distortion, symbolizing how artificial imagery erodes the authenticity of human creativity. It warns that what was once timeless and intimate becomes hollow when reduced to algorithmic output.
