Digital Illustrations

The Weeknd

This poster captures the bold, cinematic energy of The Weeknd through a flat vector style in Adobe Illustrator. The Weeknd’s features have been made using clean geometric shapes. This piece emphasizes the iconic red jacket and oversized glasses. The solid orange background heightens the contrast and vibrancy while modern. Minimal typography ensures the illustration remains the focal point. The piece demonstrates the ability to communicate personality and mood through minimal shapes and bold colors.

Drake

This poster captures the recognizable aesthetic of Drake through a flat vector style in Adobe Illustrator. Drake’s features and pose have been simplified into clean layered shapes. This piece emphasizes the oversized red jacket as a bold focal point. The pinkish purple background connects to the cultural imagery of Hotline Bling while providing strong contrast. Minimal typography balances the composition, allowing the illustration to remain the centerpiece.

SZA

This poster reflects the calm and introspective mood of SZA through a flat vector style in Adobe Illustrator. Her seated pose has been illustrated using smooth shapes that create a grounded composition. This piece emphasizes the deep blue background to establish a tranquil tone. Bold white typography highlights her name and the song title while the inclusion of the SOS tracklist adds context and depth. The design communicates both the reflective nature of the music and the artist’s visual identity.
