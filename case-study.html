Ralph Cornford Zine

How it started

This design is an accordion fold, ticket based piece inspired by the work of Raph Cornford. A structured layout and template were developed to ensure a cohesive visual composition. Additionally, a custom arcade style card box was crafted to house the tickets, enhancing both the presentation and user experience.

Sketches

The design process began with hand drawn sketches to explore various structural and visual concepts. These early drafts allowed me to experiment with the packaging layout and refine the storytelling aspect of the design.

Final Design

In the final design the accordion fold is successful showing the viewer the idea of a strand of tickets makes this piece not only succeed but also visually engaging. This piece captures a unique comic/arcade-like style that helps the design’s overall tone.
